In the spring of 2025, the US government started enforcing a registration requirement for foreign nationals who stay in the United States for thirty days or longer. The requirement had been on the books for a long time, but it hadn't really been applied to Canadians in practice. Now it was. For a seventy-eight-year-old who just wants to see the Gulf of Mexico in February, that's enough friction to say, "You know what? Maybe not this year."
