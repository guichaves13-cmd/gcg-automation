The first reason is the simplest, and it's the one people underestimate most. The heat.

Out on the plain in January and February, daytime temperatures in the forties are not unusual. There's almost no shade. There's no tree line to break the wind. The bitumen itself holds the heat late into the evening. Tyres running hot on bitumen that's sitting in the sun all day.
