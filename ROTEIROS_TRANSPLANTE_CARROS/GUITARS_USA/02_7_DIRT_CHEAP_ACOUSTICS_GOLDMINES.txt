Number one. And I'll be honest, this choice surprises a lot of people. Number one is the used, older, made-in-Japan acoustic from the nineteen seventies and eighties. Not one brand. A whole category. I want to spend one more minute on something a lot of folks get wrong, and it's the reason so many of these guitars get ignored in the first place. It's the way we test guitars in a store. So here's what I'd recommend. Ask the store if you can take a few guitars into a quieter room. Most decent stores have an acoustic room for exactly this reason.
